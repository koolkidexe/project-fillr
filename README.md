# Pothole Patrol

Pothole Patrol is a mobile-friendly app for reporting potholes to your town. Snap a photo, pin the location, and send it to the people who can fix it. It's built for Connecticut, with all of the state's towns listed and Glastonbury's contacts built in.

## What it does

- **Report in four taps.** Take a photo, pick a size, pin your location, and send.
- **Built for Connecticut.** Choose your town and whether the road is a town road or a state road. Reports go to the right office, with Glastonbury's town and state contacts ready to use.
- **Spam filtering.** Photos that aren't real road damage, like screenshots, memes and selfies, are caught before they reach anyone.
- **Repeat detection.** The same pothole photo sent more than once is flagged and escalated.
- **Accounts.** Sign up with your name, email and a password.
- **Road Heroes leaderboard.** People are ranked by how many of their reported potholes actually got filled, so the most helpful neighbors rise to the top.
